# Working in acyclic-sdk-spec

- The spec is the contract for three SDKs. Every change needs a fixture, or a test change in each SDK.
- Keep vendors.md sourced: every rule names a public URL. Mark anything without one **Unconfirmed**. Never use login-gated or NDA material.
- telemetry.md's never-recorded list is a hard rule. Do not add attributes that could carry patient data.
